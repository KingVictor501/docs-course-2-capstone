        Health and legal notice (Florida)

        • Not medical advice. Effects are predicted by a statistical model from each strain's lab results and user reviews. They are not clinical evidence, and they are not a diagnosis or treatment plan.
        • Talk to your doctor first, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have a heart or mental health condition, or take any medication. Cannabis can interact with medicines such as blood thinners, sedatives and antidepressants.
        • Florida allows medical use only. Recreational use is illegal. You need a recommendation from a qualified Florida physician and an active Medical Marijuana Use Registry ID card. Patients under 18 also need a second physician's approval and a registered caregiver.
        • Buy only from a licensed Florida Medical Marijuana Treatment Center. Do not drive or operate machinery after use.
        • Learn more at the Florida Office of Medical Marijuana Use. In an emergency, call 911. For poisoning questions, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
